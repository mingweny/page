Department of Economics · Hartfield University

I am an applied economist working at the intersection of labor markets, institutional economics, and public policy. My research examines how firms and workers adapt to technological change, with a focus on wage inequality and organizational structure.

Before joining Hartfield, I was a postdoctoral fellow at the National Bureau of Economic Research and held visiting positions at LSE and the University of Chicago Booth School of Business.
